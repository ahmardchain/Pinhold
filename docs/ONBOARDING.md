# Messages to forward

Drafts for you (@Ahmardchain) to send. Pinhold never posts or messages anyone for you.

## To a channel owner

> Hi! I'm trying something new for our ad bookings, called Pinhold. It makes sure you always get paid for ads you run, in naira.
>
> How it works: the sponsor pays upfront into Pinhold. You post the ad and leave it up for the agreed hours. Pinhold checks the post is still there, then pays you automatically in cNGN (a naira stablecoin). No chasing payments.
>
> One-time setup, about 10 minutes:
> 1. Install Valora (or another wallet that shows cNGN) and copy your wallet address.
> 2. Install the Self app and add your passport. It proves you are a real person without sharing your details.
> 3. Open the Pinhold bot on Telegram: @{bot username}. Tap Start.
> 4. Add the bot to your channel as an admin with every permission switched off. It only needs to see posts.
> 5. Send the bot: `/register @yourchannel <your wallet address> <your price in naira> <hours>`. Example: `/register @mychannel 0xAbc… 5000 24`
> 6. Open the Self link the bot sends you and scan once.
>
> When a sponsor pays, the bot sends you the exact text to post. Post it unchanged and leave it up. If the post is deleted or edited early, the sponsor gets their money back instead, so please don't touch it until the time is up.

## To an ad client (sponsor)

> For your next booking I'd like to use Pinhold. Your money sits in escrow until the post has stayed up for the full time. If the channel deletes or edits it, or never posts it, you get it back automatically.
>
> One-time setup (I can do this with you on a call):
> 1. Install the Self app and add your passport. This verifies you as a real person, so nobody can book under your name.
> 2. I'll run the Pinhold sponsor agent with you. It creates your booking agent and shows a QR code to scan with Self.
> 3. Send USD₮ on Celo to your agent's address from your own wallet or exchange. About $5 covers a booking plus a little spare.
>
> Then each booking is one command. You get a receipt link that shows the post, every check, and the payment on Celoscan.
